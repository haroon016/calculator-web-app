# Jobs To Be Done

## Job J-1 (Everyday calculation user)

"When I need to answer a day-to-day numerical question, I want to work through arithmetic with decimals, percentages, or negative values, so I can get a reliable result for the decision in front of me."

## Job J-2 (Everyday calculation user)

"When I need to evaluate a more involved mathematical expression, I want to calculate powers, roots, trigonometric values, logarithms, and grouped operations, so I can get the value of the expression I am working with."

## Job J-3 (Financial estimate user)

"When I am considering a fixed-rate loan, I want to estimate the monthly payment and total repayment from the amount, annual rate, and term, so I can plan for the expected cost in my budget."

## Job J-4 (Financial estimate user)

"When I am sharing a restaurant bill, I want to work out the tip and divide the total across the group, so I can understand each person's share."
